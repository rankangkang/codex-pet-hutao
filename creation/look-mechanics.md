# Hu Tao look mechanics
The feet, teal trouser body and small hands remain planted and forward facing. The separate oversized head naturally yaws gently toward screen-left or screen-right and pitches upward or downward around the neck. Brown glasses follow the face as one worn object; lens ellipses foreshorten with the head. Whole ivory eyeballs rotate within the lids; keep the same plum iris, dark pupil, sclera and outlines. Eyebrows, nose, beard and hair follow the head, allowing modest chin lift or tuck. Never rotate the torso or expose the back of the head. Preserve the broad beard, both glasses lenses and readable eyes throughout.
Up: face near frontal, chin lifted, irises near upper eyelids, nose tip lifted. Right: head turns toward viewer-right, nose on right of head center and eyes aim right. Down: chin visibly tucked and irises low in eye openings. Left: reverse the rightward landmark rule. Diagonal directions combine the corresponding horizontal yaw and vertical pitch; even 22.5 degree increments, with the strongest vertical component near up/down and strongest horizontal component near right/left. Keep shoes at the same baseline and head dimensions stable; only head/neck/eyes move.
